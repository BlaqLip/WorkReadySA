PRIVACY & DATA

Your information.
A clear purpose.

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

What we collect

Accounts store your name, email, account type, company (for employers), a password hash and optional profile information. Applications store your name, email, phone (if provided), note, PDF CV, selected vacancy and application status. Enquiries store your name, email and message.

How it is used

We use this information to operate accounts, accept applications, support employer review, publish reviewed vacancies and respond to enquiries. Only the relevant employer and site administrators can access a submitted application. Candidates can view and withdraw their own applications. Employers cannot browse other employers’ candidates.

Storage and security

Data is stored privately on Netlify infrastructure; storage may be outside South Africa. CVs are downloaded through an authenticated API. They are not published as public website files. Sessions use an HttpOnly cookie and passwords are salted and hashed. Uploaded PDFs are not automatically malware-scanned: employers should scan downloads before opening them.

Account and data control

You can update your profile, change your password, withdraw applications or delete your account from the workspace. Deleting a candidate account removes its applications and uploaded CVs. Deleting an employer account closes its vacancies; candidates retain their application history. You may request removal of an enquiry through the website enquiry form, quoting its reference. Administrators handle these requests through their private storage dashboard. Backups and infrastructure logs may remain according to the hosting provider’s retention.

Cookies and messages

A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. It is necessary for the account service. This app does not send automated emails, verify email ownership or offer password recovery by email. Application status updates are shown in your workspace.

Share only what is needed

Do not upload identity documents, banking details or unnecessary sensitive information. Never pay to apply. Job review is not a guarantee of an employer’s legitimacy, suitability or an employment outcome.

Questions or requests

Use the enquiry form and describe the relevant account or application. Never send your password.
