CV Tips

5 Things Every SA Job Seeker Should Include in Their CV

Stand out in a competitive market with a CV that speaks directly to South African employers.

Read guide

Five essentials for your CV

Include clear contact details, a short role-specific summary, relevant skills, education or certificates, and experience with concrete examples of your contribution. Keep formatting simple, proofread carefully and avoid unnecessary personal identifiers.

Interview Tips

How to Prepare for a Job Interview in South Africa

From what to wear to what to say — practical tips for making a great first impression.

Read guide

Prepare for your interview

Read the job description, research the employer and prepare examples of how you solved problems. Practise explaining your skills clearly, plan your journey or test your video setup, and prepare thoughtful questions about the role.

A consistent screening process

Define the skills and responsibilities of the role before reviewing candidates. Use consistent questions and job-relevant criteria. Verify information through an appropriate, consent-based process and document hiring decisions clearly.
